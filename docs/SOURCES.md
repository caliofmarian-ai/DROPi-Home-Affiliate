# Source and rights register

Research date: **2026-09-15**. Product URLs, exact variants, checked dates and factual limitations live in `data/products.json`. Supporting general references are in `data/research-sources.json`.

All 16 candidates were researched from the relevant manufacturer pages: IKEA Ireland and Joseph Joseph's European site. This establishes specification provenance for the recorded snapshot only. It does not establish stock, delivery at checkout, an affiliate relationship, permission to reproduce imagery, or a practical hands-on test.

## Important variant decisions

- UPPDATERA's nominal 32 × 50 cm label is not substituted for its recorded exact 316 × 495 mm dimensions.
- The compact Joseph Joseph organiser's 57 mm product height is not treated as sufficient drawer height: the manufacturer specifies a minimum 80 mm drawer height.
- The PÄRKLA candidate is the 55 × 49 × 30 cm variant, not a different low under-bed variant.
- The Joseph Joseph cookware organiser has slightly different prose and specification minimum widths; the more conservative 306 mm specification is recorded with an explicit caution.
- Soft storage cases can bulge. SAMLA lid-included and lid-separate products are distinct candidates. Powered devices must not be boxed merely because their dimensions fit.

## Rights boundaries

No merchant photography, videos, logos, reviews, ratings or substantial marketing copy are copied. Descriptions and guides are newly drafted and still need human editorial review. Brand/product names identify source products; no affiliation or trade-mark clearance is claimed. Generated CSS and text form the current site, so no paid image/video credits or external font licences are needed for this build.

The fit checker is geometric screening, not a structural, fire, electrical, load-bearing or installation approval. Consult the exact manufacturer instructions for any actual use.
