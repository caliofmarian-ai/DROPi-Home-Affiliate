# Future Local Services / Manufacturing Channel

Status: HOLD / DISCOVERY ONLY

This channel is intentionally separate from the current affiliate MVP. It records a possible future collaboration with a local furniture manufacturer and fitting partners without changing the present launch scope.

## Preferred first commercial model: qualified referral

DROPi Home finds and qualifies a private-customer lead. The manufacturing partner remains the seller/manufacturer, provides the final survey/quotation, accepts the order, invoices the customer, manufactures, delivers, handles defects/warranty and owns the customer sales contract. DROPi receives an agreed referral commission only after a defined commercial event such as cleared customer payment.

This model should be tested before DROPi buys/resells furniture in its own name. Acting as the reseller would move consumer-contract, refund, conformity and after-sales responsibilities onto DROPi.

## Proposed customer journey

1. Customer enters room dimensions, postcode/service area and furniture need.
2. DROPi performs a fit-first qualification and obtains consent to share the enquiry.
3. Suitable standard affiliate products are shown first where appropriate.
4. If no suitable standard product exists, the customer may request a custom quote.
5. The manufacturing partner decides whether to quote and remains responsible for technical design/manufacture.
6. A fitting option may later be offered by an approved fitter network.
7. DROPi records referral attribution and commission evidence.

## Most compatible first product opportunities

Start with products close to an existing cabinet/kitchen manufacturing workflow rather than requiring a completely new factory process:

- narrow wardrobes and storage towers;
- under-bed drawers and non-upholstered storage modules;
- wall-mounted/fold-down desks;
- hallway and utility storage;
- room-divider storage;
- compact desk/wardrobe combinations;
- bespoke infill cabinets for awkward rental/home spaces.

Upholstered furniture, children's furniture, bunk/high beds and novel transformable sleeping furniture should be treated as later specialist categories because they add different materials, structural/safety requirements and potentially new liability/production controls.

## Possible later business models

### Referral partner
Partner sells; DROPi receives a commission for an attributable paid lead/order. Lowest operational burden and preferred pilot.

### Referral + fitting coordination
Partner sells furniture; an approved fitter performs installation; DROPi earns separate agreed referral/coordination revenue. Requires clear responsibility, insurance and service-contract boundaries.

### White-label / reseller
DROPi buys at trade price and resells to the customer. Potentially higher gross margin but materially greater consumer-law, cash-flow, cancellation, warranty, delivery and after-sales responsibility. Not recommended for the first pilot.

### Co-developed compact-space range
DROPi supplies demand data and fit-first product requirements; the manufacturing partner develops its own compliant products. Commercial terms could use referral commission, wholesale pricing, revenue share or a separately agreed product-development arrangement.

## Agreement points before any real lead is sent

Obtain written employer/partner approval before operating this channel, particularly because the project owner is currently an employee of the potential manufacturing partner. The agreement should define at minimum:

- permission to run the activity outside/alongside employment duties;
- whether DROPi is referrer, agent, reseller or service coordinator;
- lead ownership and attribution;
- when commission becomes earned and payable;
- commission calculation base (for example excluding VAT, refunds and agreed pass-through charges);
- quotation and price authority;
- who signs the consumer contract and takes payment;
- design approval, measurement responsibility and change orders;
- delivery, fitting, damage, defects, warranty and after-sales responsibility;
- marketing/brand/product-photo permission;
- customer-data sharing and privacy responsibility;
- territory, exclusivity (if any), termination and treatment of leads already introduced;
- prohibition on copying third-party protected designs without permission.

## Integration with DROPi Home

Do not turn this into a separate disconnected website at first. The future funnel can be a branch from the existing fit-first experience:

`Standard product fits` → affiliate offer

`No standard product fits` → request custom quote

`Need installation` → Who Fits It

This gives one customer-acquisition engine three possible value paths while keeping each seller/service provider's responsibility explicit.
