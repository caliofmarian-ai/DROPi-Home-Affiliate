# Owner decision record — operator model and media intake

Date: 2026-09-17
Repository: `caliofmarian-ai/DROPi-Home-Affiliate`
Status: OWNER-APPROVED DESIGN DECISIONS; activation remains gated

The Project Owner approved the following decisions in conversation:

1. For the initial pilot, the intended legal operator/controller model is the Project Owner operating personally / as an Irish sole trader, subject to completing the required Revenue registration and, if the public trading name differs from the owner's true name, business-name registration with the CRO before commercial public launch.
2. A separate dedicated privacy/business email will be created by the Project Owner. The address is not yet known and must not be invented.
3. The previously proposed 30-day retention period is approved for the private **text-only pilot** where a request does not progress into a separate real contractual/business record.
4. The Project Owner approves consent as the chosen Article 6(1)(a) model for the private text-only pilot. Activation still requires final transparency wording, controller identity/contact and processor/transfer review.
5. Photo and video intake is approved as a future capability, but must be a separately gated processing operation. Media must not be enabled merely because text-only intake is approved.
6. Before a customer uploads photo/video, the interface must explain how to capture only what is necessary for the furniture request and how to protect privacy, including avoiding people, children, identity documents, mail, screens, family photographs, financial/medical information and unrelated private items. If narration is used, the customer should avoid names and unrelated private information.
7. Media requires separate affirmative acknowledgement/consent and versioned capture guidance.
8. Customer media is for request assessment / measurement support only and must remain `NO_TRAINING`.
9. Media must be deleted when the related work/request is completed or cancelled/closed. A declined request should also be closed and purged rather than retained without purpose.
10. After the media objects are confirmed purged, the customer should receive a deletion-confirmation email. No email may be claimed as sent until an email provider/account is actually configured and delivery is attempted successfully.
11. Manufacturer/employer forwarding remains OFF until a written commercial/data-sharing agreement exists.

This record does not by itself activate collection, public intake, media upload, AI processing or manufacturer routing.
