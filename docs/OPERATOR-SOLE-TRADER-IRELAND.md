# DROPi Home — Irish Sole-Trader Operator Checklist

Status: CANONICAL PRE-LAUNCH CHECKLIST / NOT LEGAL OR TAX REGISTRATION EVIDENCE
Date: 2026-09-17

## Owner decision

The initial intended operator/controller is the Project Owner personally, operating as an Irish sole trader, rather than inventing a company before the business is validated.

This is an operational starting model, not a claim that sole-trader status is always preferable. A sole trader does **not** have the liability separation of a limited company. Incorporation may be reconsidered later if sales, product liability, staffing, finance, investment or contractual risk justify it.

## Before commercial public launch

The repository must not mark the public-commercial operator gate as complete until real evidence exists for each applicable item.

1. **Revenue registration / tax setup**
   - Establish the real sole-trader tax registration required for the activity.
   - Keep the resulting real tax identifiers/evidence outside public GitHub; store only the minimum non-secret status/evidence reference needed by project governance.

2. **Trading / business name**
   - If trading publicly as `DROPi Home` rather than solely under the owner's true personal name, complete the Irish business-name registration required by the CRO before relying on that identity publicly.
   - Business-name registration does not create a separate company or limited liability.

3. **Controller identity**
   - Final privacy notice must state the real controller/operator identity.
   - Do not publish placeholder company names or invented registration numbers.

4. **Geographic/contact address**
   - Decide a lawful business/contact address suitable for the legal notices that require a geographic address.
   - Do not use a fake address. If the owner does not want a residential address exposed publicly, assess a legitimate business/service-address arrangement before launch.

5. **Dedicated privacy/business email**
   - Owner will create a separate address.
   - The address must be verified before `privacyContactStatus` may become `VERIFIED`.

6. **Tax/VAT review per revenue stream**
   - Affiliate commissions, direct/dropship sales and future service/referral income must not be treated as the same tax transaction automatically.
   - Counterparty and VAT treatment must be reviewed using the real provider/company details before monetisation gates pass.

7. **Consumer-facing role clarity**
   - Affiliate pages must make clear that the retailer is the seller where that is the real arrangement.
   - Dropshipping/direct-sale checkout must remain disabled until DROPi's seller obligations are satisfied.
   - Future custom-furniture manufacturer referrals must state who contracts with the customer and who is responsible for quotation, manufacturing, delivery, fitting, remedies and warranties.

## Data-controller implications

For Custom Furniture Intake, the sole trader/controller is responsible for deciding and documenting:

- purpose and lawful basis;
- data minimisation;
- transparency wording;
- processors and transfers;
- access/rectification/erasure/restriction handling;
- text/media retention;
- security and authorised access;
- breach handling;
- recipient/manufacturer sharing when later enabled.

Using Railway, Neon or another processor does not transfer the controller's GDPR responsibility to the provider.
