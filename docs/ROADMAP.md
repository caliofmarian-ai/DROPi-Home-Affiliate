## First commercial validation

The candidate niche is a hypothesis, not proven demand. Identify a few specific queries and compare their usefulness and realistic eligible affiliate offers. Replace or drop candidates lacking accessible programme coverage. Do not manufacture affiliate status for a visually convenient product. A small useful site with authentic decisions is preferred to mass autogenerated articles.

After publishing, log actual visitors, outbound clicks where lawful, approved conversions, reversals, payout receipts and operating costs. Decide whether to continue, change niche, or stop based on measured contribution. The hypothetical calculator is not that measurement system.
